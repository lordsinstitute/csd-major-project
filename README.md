FinancePilot: Generative AI-Powered Personal Financial Advisory Platform

Project Overview

FinancePilot is a Generative AI-powered personal financial advisory platform designed to help individuals manage their finances in a simple and effective way.

The platform provides personalized financial suggestions based on users’ income, expenses, spending habits, and financial goals. It aims to improve financial awareness, encourage responsible money management, and make personal financial planning easier and more accessible.

Problem Statement

Many individuals face difficulties managing their income, expenses, savings, investments, and future financial needs due to limited financial knowledge or access to personalized financial guidance.

FinancePilot addresses these challenges by providing AI-powered financial insights and recommendations.

Objectives

* Analyze users’ income and expenses.
* Identify spending patterns and unnecessary expenses.
* Suggest suitable budgets and savings strategies.
* Provide personalized financial recommendations.
* Assist users in planning future financial goals.
* Promote financial awareness and responsible money management.

Key Features

* Expense Analysis: Analyze spending patterns and identify unnecessary expenses.
* Budget Management: Suggest suitable budgets based on income and spending habits.
* Savings Recommendations: Provide suggestions to improve savings.
* Personalized Financial Guidance: Generate recommendations based on individual financial information.
* Financial Goal Planning: Help users plan for:
    * Emergency funds
    * Education expenses
    * Vehicle purchases
    * Future financial needs
